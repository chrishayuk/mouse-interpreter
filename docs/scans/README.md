# Scans of the original sources

Page images from the Internet Archive's scans of BYTE magazine, reduced in
size. They are here so that the interpreter can be checked against what was
actually printed.

| Folder | Source |
|--------|--------|
| `byte-1979-07/` | Peter Grogono, "Mouse: A Language for Microcomputers", BYTE, July 1979, pp. 198-220. Archive item `byte-magazine-1979-07`. |
| `byte-1980-06/` | Tom Lane, "Comment and Correction for Mouse", with Peter Grogono's reply, BYTE, June 1980, pp. 238 and 240. Archive item `byte-magazine-1980-06`. |
| `details/` | Full-resolution crops: lines 169-179 of listing 6 (p. 212), and Figure 1 (p. 216). |

Where to look:

- Listing 6, the Pascal interpreter: pp. 209, 210 and 212.
- Line 176, printed with a minus sign that should be a plus: p. 212.
- Figure 1, the stack while a parameter is passed through two macros: p. 216.
- Listings 1 and 2: p. 206. Listings 3, 4 and 5: p. 208.

The article pages also carry unrelated advertisements. BYTE magazine is
copyright its publishers; these pages are reproduced for reference and
research. Check that you are comfortable with that before publishing this
folder.
